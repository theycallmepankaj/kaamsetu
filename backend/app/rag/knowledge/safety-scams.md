title: Staying safe from job scams
source: https://cybercrime.gov.in
audience: all

## Signs of a fake job offer
- Anyone asking for money to give you a job (registration fee, uniform fee, "security deposit") is almost always a scam. Real employers do not charge workers to hire them.
- Offers of very high pay for very easy work.
- Asking you to share an OTP, your UPI PIN, bank password or to install a screen-sharing app.
- Pressure to decide immediately.
KaamSetu never asks for your OTP, UPI PIN, bank password or Aadhaar number in chat.

## What to do if you are cheated online
Call the national cyber crime helpline 1930 immediately (the faster you call, the better the chance of stopping the money) and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Keep screenshots of messages and payment details.
Keywords: 1930, cyber, complaint, shikayat, शिकायत, paise kat gaye, money lost, UPI fraud

## Safety when going to a new job
- Share the job address and employer name with a family member.
- Use the "On the way" and "Arrived" buttons in KaamSetu so there is a record.
- Agree on the wage and number of days in the KaamSetu chat before starting work.
- Women workers can call the women helpline 181 in any emergency, and anyone can call 112.
