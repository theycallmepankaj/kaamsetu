## Construction worker registration (BOCW)
Every state has a Building and Other Construction Workers (BOCW) Welfare Board. Construction workers such as masons, helpers, painters, carpenters, electricians, plumbers and welders working on building sites can register.
Usual conditions: age 18 to 60 years, and at least 90 days of construction work in the last 12 months (an employer, contractor or union can certify this).
Benefits differ by state and can include: education help for children, maternity help, accident help, marriage help, tool kits, housing help and pension. Check your state's BOCW board website or the district labour office for the exact benefits and how to apply.
