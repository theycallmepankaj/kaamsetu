## What is e-Shram
e-Shram is the Government of India's national database of unorganised workers, run by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. Construction workers, domestic workers, drivers, street vendors, farm workers, gig and platform workers (such as delivery partners) and other informal workers can register. After registering you get an e-Shram card with a Universal Account Number (UAN). Registration helps the government reach you with social security and welfare schemes.

## Who can register on e-Shram
- Age between 16 and 59 years.
- You work in the unorganised sector (no regular job with PF or ESIC).
- You are not a member of EPFO or ESIC.
- You do not pay income tax.

## How to register on e-Shram
Registration is free. Never pay an agent for it.
1. Keep ready: Aadhaar card, the mobile number linked to Aadhaar, and your bank account details.
2. Register yourself at eshram.gov.in using an Aadhaar OTP, or visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC), where an operator can help.
3. Fill in your occupation, skills, address and education, then download your e-Shram card.
The e-Shram helpline number is 14434.
