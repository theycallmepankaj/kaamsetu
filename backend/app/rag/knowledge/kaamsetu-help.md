## Creating your worker profile
Go to "My profile". Tap the microphone and describe yourself in your own words, for example: "I am an electrician with 5 years of experience in Indore, I charge 800 rupees a day". KaamSetu fills in your skills, experience, city and daily wage. Check them, change anything that is wrong, and tap Save. Allow location so you get jobs near you.
Keywords: profile, profile kaise banaye, प्रोफाइल, voice, bolkar, बोलकर, mic, skills, kaam

## Finding and accepting jobs (workers)
Jobs that match your skills near you appear on your home screen. When a new job is posted nearby you get a live alert. Tap "Apply" to show interest. When the employer hires you, you get an alert with their contact number, and a chat opens. Use "On the way" and "Arrived" to update the employer.
Keywords: job, kaam, काम, naukri, नौकरी, apply, alert, nearby, paas, पास, hire

## Posting a job (employers)
Tap "Post a job". Choose the skill, write what work is needed, set the daily wage, number of days and how many workers you need, and allow location. Nearby available workers with that skill get an instant alert. You see applications live, can check each worker's experience and rating, and hire with one tap. Mark the work as completed when it is done, and rate the worker.
Keywords: post job, hire worker, employer, malik, मालिक, worker chahiye, kaamgar, कामगार, painter chahiye, mistri chahiye

## Ratings
After work is completed, both the worker and the employer can rate each other from 1 to 5 stars. Good ratings help workers get more jobs and help employers find workers faster.
Keywords: rating, review, star, stars
