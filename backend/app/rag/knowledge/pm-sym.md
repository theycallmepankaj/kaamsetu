## What is PM-SYM
Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-dhan (PM-SYM) is a voluntary pension scheme of the Government of India for unorganised workers. After you turn 60, you receive an assured pension of ₹3,000 per month. If the subscriber dies after pension starts, the spouse receives 50% of the pension as family pension.

## Who can join PM-SYM
- Unorganised workers aged 18 to 40 years at the time of joining.
- Monthly income of ₹15,000 or less.
- Not a member of EPFO, ESIC or NPS, and not an income tax payer.

## How much you pay for PM-SYM
You pay a small monthly contribution from ₹55 to ₹200, depending on your age when you join (younger people pay less). The government adds the same amount as you pay. Contributions are auto-debited from your savings bank or Jan Dhan account.
To join, visit a Common Service Centre (CSC) with your Aadhaar card and savings bank or Jan Dhan account details.
